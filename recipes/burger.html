Burger

Description

This burger will have a beef patty, lettuce, tomato, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • Beef patty
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Season the beef patty with salt and pepper.
  3. Place the patty on the grill and cook for 4-5 minutes on each side.
  4. Toast the buns on the grill for 1 minute.
  5. Assemble the burger with the cooked patty and toppings.

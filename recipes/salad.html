Salad

Description

This salad will have mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, and a vinaigrette dressing.

Ingredients

  • Mixed greens
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Vinaigrette dressing

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the mixed greens.
  2. Halve the cherry tomatoes.
  3. Whisk together the vinaigrette ingredients.
  4. Toss the greens and tomatoes with the vinaigrette.
  5. Serve immediately.

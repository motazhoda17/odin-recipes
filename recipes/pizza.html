Pizza

Description

This pizza will have cheese, sauce, bread

Ingredients

  • Cheese
  • Sauce
  • Bread

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C)
  2. Roll out the dough on a floured surface
  3. Transfer the dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet
  4. Spread the sauce evenly over the dough
  5. Top with cheese and other toppings
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown
